# COMET 1.0.0 beta 3

- Squads can split roles during a fight. One bot throws a grenade while a teammate holds an angle. They can also watch a building's exits when they have enough teammates to cover them.
- PMCs take recognized bosses, guards and faction patrols into account when deciding whether to push.
- Dark corners can buy you or another bot more time before being spotted. Bare bushes offer less cover in autumn and winter, while solid tree trunks still count.
- After an emergency reload, bots can return for their dropped magazine. Reloading and choosing compatible ammo are also more reliable.
- Bots share lighting readings for the same target. Performance reports now show the busiest frame and how often lighting checks exceeded the budget of one new bot reading per frame.
