# COMET 1.0.0 beta 3

- **A squad can wait you out.** If enough squadmates can watch a building's exits, they can hold outside until you emerge. During a firefight, one can throw a grenade from cover while another holds the angle.
- **One guard can be enough to make a PMC think twice.** Early in a raid, a cautious PMC expects a boss or faction patrol to have backup. A more aggressive PMC may still push. As the raid wears on, a lone survivor becomes more plausible.
- **A dark corner can buy you a moment.** Bots take longer to spot you or another bot in the shade. A bot that just saw you can still recognize you on the next peek. In autumn and winter, bots pass up bare bushes for cover and can still shelter behind a solid trunk.
- **Watch where that magazine lands.** After an emergency reload, a bot can come back for its mag once the fight dies down. Reloads and ammo selection are also more reliable.

Bots share lighting checks for the same target. The performance report shows how often those checks exceed one new bot reading per frame.
