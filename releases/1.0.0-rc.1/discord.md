☄️ **COMET 1.0.0 RC1**

**What's changed since Beta 4**

- Squads send one PMC to flank while teammates cover. Once the flanker gets into position or spots the target, the squad can push together.
- Bots can route around open door panels. When they move to new cover outdoors, they hold it for a few seconds before making their next move.
- After a fight, PMCs can reload on the way to cover or while catching up with their squad leader, even if the leader has moved on.

**Fixes**

- PMCs count the round in the chamber when deciding whether they have enough ammo for a squad push or to fire around a corner.
- Fixed cases where a bot planned a throw without a usable grenade or kept a peek going after its movement was refused.

I've spotted a few more things I'd like to improve and a few small bugs to fix. If RC1 goes well, those will be in the official 1.0.0 release. Let me know how your raids go 🙂
