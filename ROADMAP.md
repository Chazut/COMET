# COMET roadmap

## Ideas to explore

- **PMCs with bolt-action rifles.** Look into reports of PMCs rushing into close quarters or strafing through shots when they already have a clear shot at range. Compare scoped rifles and iron sights with the current preferred-range settings. Would these PMCs fight more convincingly if they held their distance and stopped briefly to aim, while still moving when under pressure?
- **Let an unaware scav pass.** A PMC could hold fire when a scav hasn't spotted them. A cautious PMC carrying valuable loot would be more likely to let the scav walk by. If the scav opens fire on them, the PMC can shoot back.
